# Concept 02 — Tropical Editorial (2026-11-05)

## Creative direction
**Thesis:** A modern Southwest Florida outdoor renovation brand with architectural composition and the warmth of tropical, lived-in spaces.

**Audience:** Homeowners, landscapers and contractors considering sod purchases, installation, artificial turf, pavers, tree work and outdoor improvements across Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Estero, Bonita Springs, Naples and Lehigh Acres.

**Objective:** Increase qualified estimate inquiries and clearly distinguish sod-by-the-pallet requests from installation estimates.

## Story & journey
1. **Hero:** Who we are, what we offer, where we work. Two explicit CTAs.
2. **Approach:** Why the right preparation/material matters without fabricated accolades.
3. **Services:** Five primary services in editorial photographic grid, secondary services as compact text links.
4. **Sod/soil:** Sod-by-pallet purchase inquiries with the three existing varieties; price/delivery are confirmed, not invented.
5. **Decision support:** Natural sod vs manufactured turf considerations and service-specific links.
6. **Work:** Three genuinely sourced project images with service links; no invented project metrics or before/after claims.
7. **Social:** Existing small thumbnail/social-brand showcase retained.
8. **Process:** Three user-centered practical steps, not a warranty or completion promise.
9. **FAQs:** Visible answers to buyer questions; no unsupported FAQ schema.
10. **Close:** Quote CTA, direct telephone and verified address.

## Brand system
- Selected palm-and-field logo asset is kept; future update requires approved clean vector dark/light versions.
- Palette: Forest #0E3B34; Deep #072A23; Teal #0F5F5B; Orange #EF7626; Equipment red #B53A2E; Ivory #F7F3E8; Sage #DCE8D8.
- Type: existing locally optimized DM Sans and Playfair Display, scaled editorially.
- Grid: spacious 1320px shell, asymmetric hero/photo grids and image-led sections, mobile deliberate 1- and 2-column patterns.
- Motion: restrained image scale and button movement; disabled for reduced-motion preference.
- Accessibility: semantic sections/H1, accessible links and mobile menu, contrast, visible focus, meaningful image alt labels.

## Technical approach
Reuses Next.js App Router on Vercel, existing service pages, data model, contact and sod inquiry flows, SEO metadata/schema, sitemap/robots, social media and secure photo-upload implementation. Scope of concept changes: homepage, isolated concept stylesheet, shared mobile menu and automated regression checks. No DNS or third-party credential changes.
