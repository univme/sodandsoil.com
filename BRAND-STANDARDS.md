# Express Turf Renovation LLC — Brand Standards

## Identity
Use the approved palm-and-field logo. Forest Green #0E3B34, Deep Teal #0F5F5B, Turf Orange #EF7626, Equipment Red #B53A2E, Warm Ivory #F7F3E8, Soft Sage #DCE8D8.

## Typography
Playfair Display for editorial emphasis; DM Sans for body and interface.

## Applications
Use dark green wordmarks on light backgrounds and light wordmarks on forest green. Retain ample clear space. Do not distort, stretch or recolor the icon.

The current repository contains a small-resolution cropped preview from the user-supplied light logo sheet; replace it with approved transparent vector and high-resolution dark/light masters before production.
